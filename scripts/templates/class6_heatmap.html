Colors describe the predicate, not overall eligibility. Green means “met.” For an exclusion, green means the exclusion is present and may count against candidacy. Grey means unknown, not a failed requirement. All results require human review.

Eligibility evidence score = verified passing criteria ÷ all applicable required criteria × 100. Unknown and unverified results earn no points; justified not-applicable criteria are excluded. This is not an eligibility probability. An explicit eligibility conflict takes precedence over any score.
